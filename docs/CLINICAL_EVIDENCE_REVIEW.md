# Clinical utility and evidence review

29 October 2026. Intended use: supervised adult psychological assessment in an
Australian private clinical/forensic practice. This is a source-based product
review, not an independent clinical validation, legal opinion or endorsement.
The combined pathway has not been validated in this practice population.

## What makes the current app useful

Use it to organise a referral-specific assessment, identify areas needing further
enquiry, collect intact questionnaires, document safety assessment and follow-up,
and preserve the actual rules and source of each result. An overall pass/fail,
diagnosis or risk probability is not supported. Choose relevant domains rather
than routinely administering every available questionnaire.

| Clinical question | Current support | Decision boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Depression and generalised anxiety | PHQ-2/9 and GAD-2/7; full-form monitoring; separate functioning/context notes | Follow with interview, impairment, course and differential diagnosis. GAD scores do not screen every anxiety disorder. |
| Trauma symptoms | Exposure, PC-PTSD-5, then PCL-5; below-gate clinician override | A symptom-cluster pattern is provisional. Establish qualifying exposure and the remaining diagnostic criteria clinically. |
| Alcohol and substance use | AUDIT-C/AUDIT; optional TAPS with separate substance-class results | Assess intoxication, withdrawal and overdose independently. Original TAPS alcohol examples use US drinks, not Australian standard drinks. |
| ADHD and dissociation | Unmodified ASRS six-item screener; DSS-B | ASRS prompts assessment, not diagnosis. DSS-B has no established automatic cutoff or full-DSS gate. |
| Personality/formulation | Optional Mini-IPIP, IPIP-NEO-120 and selected complete CAT-PD traits | Raw traits are not personality-disorder, psychopathy, credibility or offending-risk determinations. |
| Suicide, violence, safeguarding and medical concerns | Direct safety disposition/method/outcome, follow-up ownership/date; external instruments and local interview notes | These are not validated predictive risk scores. A negative symptom screen cannot close the enquiry. |
| Forensic referral | Referral/purpose/recipient, administration conditions, collateral, account/observations and limitations | A clinical diagnosis or symptom score does not answer a legal capacity, responsibility or violence-risk question. |

The current public catalogue does not expose the historical mania, eating,
perinatal, cognitive or partner-violence card definitions as administered modules. They must not be
counted as included screens. Use direct assessment, the existing formulation/
context notes and authorised external services; the guide identifies these gaps.
The historical definitions also received clinician-copy corrections so lower-level
legacy consumers do not inherit outdated research-floor claims.

## Threshold and population audit

The implemented PHQ-2 gate of 2 is a sensitivity-first alternative to the
publisher's brief cutoff of 3. Importantly, its accuracy is not the accuracy of
the complete two-stage pathway. Levis et al.'s semistructured-interview subset
reported sensitivity 0.82 and specificity 0.87 for PHQ-2 at 2 followed by PHQ-9 at
10. These are pooled study estimates, not local performance. Do not promise a
universal sensitivity floor or multiply stage sensitivities as if independent.
[Primary individual-participant meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7284301/).

The GAD gate is also sensitivity-first. The USPSTF's population-screening
recommendation concerns adults without a diagnosed disorder or recognised
symptoms; evidence for routine screening from age 65 is insufficient. This is
not a ban on clinically indicated assessment of older adults. Do not apply the
recommendation as proof of accuracy in a referred, comorbid or forensic sample.
[Recommendation and population](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/anxiety-adults-screening).

VA guidance reports PC-PTSD-5 at 4 balanced errors overall and in men in a VA
primary-care sample; lower cuts may be appropriate when sensitivity is the aim.
The app's gate of 3 remains a documented choice. PCL-5 total thresholds around
31 to 33 depend on population/purpose; its cluster algorithm does not establish
all PTSD criteria. DSS-B cut points need further research. No new cutoff was
introduced in this review.
[PC-PTSD-5](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/professional/assessment/screens/pc-ptsd.asp),
[PCL-5](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/professional/assessment/adult-sr/ptsd-checklist.asp),
[DSS-B](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/professional/assessment/adult-sr/dss.asp).

For ADHD, include developmental history, impairment across settings, collateral
where available and competing explanations. A rating scale alone is inadequate.
The Australian guideline describes case identification and diagnostic assessment;
the included screener does not substitute for that process.
[Australian guideline development group](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10363932/),
[NICE NG87, section 1.3](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng87/chapter/Recommendations).

## Safety is a clinical pathway, not another symptom gate

NICE NG225 advises against predicting suicide/repeated self-harm with scales or
global low/medium/high categories, or using them to decide treatment/discharge.
Use the person's needs, direct enquiry, formulation and an actionable safety
plan. This guidance concerns self-harm care; it is not an Australian statutory
rule or evidence that every screening instrument is useless.
[NICE NG225, section 1.6](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng225/chapter/recommendations).

C-SSRS endorsement, the official form's triage result and clinical disposition
are different things. The app's external yes/no note records endorsement only;
it cannot reconstruct the form's item/timeframe/behaviour-specific triage. Record
the actual form/version, findings, timing, collateral and resulting plan in the
safety method/outcome and follow-up fields. An externally entered PHQ total, or EPDS findings recorded in clinical notes,
cannot supply item-level self-harm responses or automatically open another form.
[Publisher's FAQ](https://cssrs.columbia.edu/the-columbia-scale-c-ssrs/faq/).

NIMH's adult outpatient model separates a brief suicide screen, a trained
clinician's brief safety assessment, then disposition. It requires a response
plan before screening starts. Its US medical-setting guidance is useful for
workflow design, but does not validate Australian forensic deployment. Do not
copy US emergency numbers into local care pathways.
[ASQ toolkit](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-conducted-at-nimh/asq-toolkit-materials),
[adult outpatient assessment guide](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-conducted-at-nimh/asq-toolkit-materials/adult-outpatient/adult-outpatient-brief-suicide-safety-assessment-guide).

Do not wait for a questionnaire threshold when the interview suggests mania,
psychosis, dangerous withdrawal, severe eating-related medical problems or an
immediate safety concern. NICE advises clinical enquiry/referral rather than
bipolar questionnaires in primary care; possible first psychosis needs timely
specialist assessment. SCOFF alone cannot determine whether an eating disorder
is present. Withdrawal seizures/delirium need medical care, independent of AUDIT.
[Bipolar CG185](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg185/chapter/recommendations),
[psychosis CG178](https://www.nice.org.uk/Guidance/CG178/chapter/recommendations),
[eating disorders NG69](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng69/chapter/Recommendations),
[alcohol complications CG100](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg100/chapter/recommendations).

For perinatal presentations, use the appropriate dedicated pathway. COPE advises
immediate further assessment when EPDS question 10 is endorsed; a total score
cannot reveal that item response. The app does not administer EPDS.
[Australian COPE guideline summary](https://www.cope.org.au/health-professionals/health-professional-information-hubs/for-obstetricians/guideline-summary).

## Forensic usefulness and limits

Document the actual psycholegal referral and distinguish self-report,
observations, collateral and uncorroborated claims. Consider alternative
explanations, language/cultural fit, disability, response conditions and the
limits of transporting clinical cutoffs. The app's forensic fields support this
documentation; selecting Forensic does not create forensic norms.
[APA specialty guidelines, sections 9 and 10](https://www.apa.org/practice/guidelines/forensic-psychology.html).

IPIP response distributions are not performance/symptom validity tests.
Neuropsychological validity assessment requires established methods and
interpretation, not a home-made malingering probability. HCR-20 V3 is a separate
structured professional judgement framework using formulation, scenarios and
management planning; it is not a patient self-report questionnaire or a sum of
Open Screen's anger/mistrust traits. Its publisher sells professional materials;
this review established no electronic redistribution grant.
[AACN validity consensus](https://doi.org/10.1080/13854046.2021.1896036),
[HCR-20 V3 publisher](https://www.parinc.com/products/HCR-20-V3).

For Victorian family-violence work, review the role-appropriate MARAM pathway
rather than treating a generic harm flag as a complete family-violence assessment.
MARAM combines victim-survivor self-assessment, evidence-based factors, relevant
shared information and professional judgement; victim-survivor and person-using-
violence assessment are distinct. Current AHPRA guidance also addresses privacy
and avoiding unsafe discussion with a person suspected of using violence. No
MARAM instrument, automated risk category or information-sharing authority is
implemented here; determine training, role, permissions and applicable practice
requirements separately.
[Victorian MARAM guides](https://www.vic.gov.au/maram-practice-guides-and-resources),
[MARAM assessment model](https://www.vic.gov.au/maram-framework-summary-organisational-leaders),
[AHPRA practitioner guidance](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Resources/Family-domestic-and-sexual-violence/Guidance-for-practitioners).

## Licensing findings and next additions

Availability, copyright ownership, clinical-use permission and permission to
redistribute an electronic form are separate questions. Existing instrument
versions/keys remain in MEASURES.md and the source manifest. No new questionnaire
items or scoring algorithm were added by this review.

| Candidate or existing instrument | Primary permission finding | Product decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C-SSRS | Publisher explicitly allows healthcare/community use and EHR embedding without special permission. Copyright remains. First select the form/timeframes and local response pathway; retain attribution and test every skip/acuity state. External access remains available now. |

Verify the exact asset and applicable notice before embedding. |
| K6/K10 | Author says use is free without formal approval; retain copyright and the requested citation. [Author terms/forms](https://rckessler.scholars.harvard.edu/k10-and-k6-scales) | Strong next candidate for optional nonspecific distress assessment/monitoring. Choose one defined form/response coding; K6 and K10 are not an established automatic two-stage diagnostic pathway. Not public domain. |

The TAPS library endpoint timed out during this follow-up. Its existing source
and permission record were not replaced by an assumption or a new blanket
licence. The Psychology Board code page returned HTTP 403; this review does not

prevents the source-specific clinical findings above. No permission request,
purchase, publisher contact or data transfer was made.

## Practice adoption and evaluation

Before practice use, an independent psychologist should audit the exact forms,
worked boundary/omission/safety cases and reports in acceptance/CLINICAL_REVIEW.md.
Agree a response pathway and responsibility before administering risk questions.
Use a small supervised pilot to assess completion, missingness, accessibility,
review time, referral appropriateness and patient burden. Compare results with
independent assessment for both screen-positive and screen-negative participants;
checking only positives cannot estimate missed cases. Determine sample size and
consent/governance with appropriate clinical/research expertise before collecting
patient data. Report subgroup uncertainty instead of inventing local accuracy.

For repeat assessment, keep the same form, timeframe and scoring method. A raw
change is not automatically reliable or clinically meaningful. Do not apply one
instrument's change threshold to another, or compare a brief score directly with
a longer form. The current per-form charts and accessible tables support this;
they intentionally do not calculate a universal reliable-change index.

Priority order is a product judgement: strengthen the independent safety
workflow first; add one permitted broad-distress option if it serves the referral;
then functioning and targeted condition-specific modules with exact permission
and evidence. Additional trait scales are lower priority than closing those
clinical workflow gaps. Human/clinical acceptance and live Praxi integration
remain outstanding.

Future questionnaire admission should use an exact reviewed definition, its
original form/period/codes, a retained rights notice, independent scoring cases,
missingness/skip tests and a clinically reviewed response pathway. A permission
for one named instrument must not widen the global licence allowlist or permit
unrelated copyrighted forms. New normative, diagnostic or risk claims require
separate evidence; a configurable threshold does not create it.
