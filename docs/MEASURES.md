# Questionnaire expansion · reviewed 26 October 2026

Nine optional questionnaires are registered in `questionnaires/registry.js`. Default selections remain depression, anxiety and trauma. Every selected brief screen precedes TAPS follow-ups and longer personality profiles. No result diagnoses a condition, clears a safety concern, establishes malingering or estimates forensic risk.

## Administration and interpretation

TAPS retains the **US form**. The clinician explicitly chooses the official male (5 drinks) or female (4 drinks) alcohol question and confirms that the form and drink sizes suit this administration. No identity is inferred. US drink examples represent about 14 g of alcohol ([NIAAA](https://www.niaaa.nih.gov/alcohols-effects-health/what-standard-drink)), rather than Australia's 10 g standard drink. The app does not relabel those examples or claim Australian validation. The form choice and acknowledgement are recorded and cleared for each new patient. TAPS and AUDIT can both be selected deliberately; they are separately scored and their responses are never substituted for one another.

TAPS-1 stores original response codes: Daily or Almost Daily = 0, Weekly = 1, Monthly = 2, Less Than Monthly = 3, Never = 4. These are **not a summed symptom score**. Any response other than Never opens the corresponding TAPS-2 class group: tobacco; alcohol; illicit drugs (cannabis, cocaine/methamphetamine, heroin, other drugs); prescription misuse (opioids, sedatives, stimulants). Within a class, recent use No skips the remaining questions. Scored classes count Yes = 1, No = 0: 0–3 per class except alcohol 0–4. See the [NIH data dictionary](https://nda.nih.gov/data-structure/taps01) and [clinical-use skip analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5377907/).

A negative past-year screen or recent-use response yields zero **by the published stop rule**, explicitly labelled in the result; a missing response never becomes zero. Earlier responses outside a changed branch remain in the opt-in response export but do not contribute to current scores. Other drug use is recorded with a description and prompts direct review; it has no invented 0–3 score. The original form does not cover every contemporary substance or use pattern, and a zero cannot clear withdrawal, intoxication or overdose concerns. The UI uses descriptive follow-up advice, not an automatic substance-use-disorder diagnosis or an aggregate risk category. No one threshold is applied across all classes: opioid use can warrant review at 1, for example ([NIDA clinical decision support report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6966783/)).

DSS-B is not gated by a PTSD score. Its eight items remain available independently, and a high score does not itself diagnose a dissociative disorder. The VA states that cut points require further research. It would be misleading to fabricate a recognised DSS-B→DSS-20 gate.

Personality inventories are formulation aids, independent of clinical symptom gates. All selected items are administered in stage two, and each completed scale is shown independently if another scale is incomplete. No missing-item imputation or prorating is performed. A profile has `total: null` and `max: null`; `scales` contains each actual sum, possible range, keyed mean and completeness. Partial domains do not receive a mean. IPIP-NEO facets are disclosed separately to keep the main report compact; print includes them. No volunteer-reference percentiles from the existing five-item tool are transferred to these inventories. The intact IPIP-NEO includes US political-attitude items; those items are not renamed into Australian political categories. Multiple personality questionnaires may be selected, but responses are not deduplicated between different forms with different administration contexts.

The selected CAT-PD trait modules are not the complete 216-item assessment, its validity scales, a personality-disorder diagnosis, a psychopathy measure or a violence-risk instrument. The hosted item fragments and keys are preserved; the instructions identify general self-description and use the original True/Untrue anchors. Scores are raw traits with no invented norms. Internal consistency estimates in the source do not establish a clinical cutoff.

## Developer contract

The public factory remains DOM-, storage- and network-free. Bundled definitions are deeply immutable, and the fixed-form validator compares against an independent snapshot rather than a caller-mutable JSON module. Import `questionnaires` from `questionnaires/registry.js` and pass it to `createScreener({ questionnaires })` to obtain the same optional measures as the browser. Bare `createScreener()` retains the established built-ins for compatibility. Register only the definitions relevant to the host; bundling a form does not select it automatically.

Additive definitions may optionally declare `scales` (explicit item-ID lists, optional domain/facet level) and `phase`. Profiles cannot also declare a grand-total screen threshold, severity bands or an automatic gate. `formChoices` declares mutually exclusive instrument variants; `setup.forms[questionnaireId]` contains the explicitly selected variant. Unknown variants are rejected; a missing required choice prevents administration. TAPS and ASRS use explicit, enumerated adapters rather than a configuration expression language. These named adapters require the unchanged reviewed JSON definition. ASRS's specific NYU permission is handled narrowly and does not weaken the general open-licence validator for unrelated definitions.

Practice preferences exclude locked published algorithms and profiles without clinical cutoffs. Other existing editable gates keep their documented before/after provenance and reset workflow. Adding this registry changes the reviewed base signature; saved preferences against an older registry require review/reset rather than silently applying to new measures. Active sittings already loaded in a tab retain their own configuration. Version 0.3.0 adds optional `forms` input and derived `scales`, `continuousTotal`, `algorithm`, `screenAt` and `license` fields. API schema 1 remains compatible for existing scalar consumers; hosts opting into profiles must handle their explicit nullable total/range and scale-specific results.

Run `npm run check` and `npm run test:e2e`. New tests cover ASRS discrepant-method and endorsement boundaries, TAPS variants/skip rules/missingness/stale branches, raw-response privacy, trait reversal and source keys, completeness, source hashes, reports, keyboard/touch advancement, patient reset, phone reflow and axe checks. A passing test suite verifies the implemented rules, not clinical validity of this application or calibration to the practice's patient population.
