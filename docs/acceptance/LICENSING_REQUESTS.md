## Common request text

Subject: Permission for electronic administration and scoring in Open Screen / Praxi

We are developing Open Screen, an adult psychology screening application intended
for use by a psychology practice and integration with the Praxi practice-management
platform. We seek written permission for the exact instrument/forms identified
below, including commercial clinical use where applicable. Please confirm the
rights holder and the scope and terms available for:

- Reproducing original English item text, instructions, anchors and scoring keys
  in browser/phone/tablet interfaces; explicit one-item-at-a-time administration.
- Automated original scoring and branching, clinician-requested follow-ups and
  configurable practice choices where permitted. Published fixed algorithms will
  remain intact; any permitted deviation will be explicit in provenance.
- Local encrypted sitting files, authenticated clinical storage in Praxi, and
  clinician summaries/HTML/PDF reports, including optional response appendices.
- Software bundling/distribution, hosted or offline use, private practice and any
  paid product, and sublicensing to authorised customer practices if needed.
- Required notices/attribution, permitted translations/accessibility adaptations,
  territory, term, usage limits, fees, renewal and evidence of permission.

Please identify any restricted uses or changes requiring additional approval and
provide the authorised form/version and scoring manual. We will not embed the
items until the required permission is documented and the form/scoring is reviewed.

## Instrument-specific additions and routes

SCI-02 and SCI-8: request both exact forms, shared-item mapping, original scoring
and evidence/permission for a two-stage pathway. Higher scores indicate better
sleep. Start from the rights information in the
[development paper](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/4/3/e004183); verify the current
rights-holder contact before sending. Non-commercial availability does not itself
establish the intended product rights.

OCI-4 and OCI-R: request electronic/commercial reproduction of both forms, official
four-item/full-form mapping, scoring and original threshold evidence. Start from
[the author paper hosted by IOCDF](https://iocdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/abramovitch_et_al_2021_the_oci-4.pdf)
and verify the rights holder. Availability of a PDF is not a redistribution grant.

WHODAS 2.0: identify the 12-item adult self-report English electronic form and
whether 36-item/interviewer/proxy versions are also wanted. Request simple and/or
IRT scoring rights, authorised code set, missingness and attribution. Use the
[WHO instrument page](https://www.who.int/standards/classifications/international-classification-of-functioning-disability-and-health/who-disability-assessment-schedule)
and its electronic-use licensing route.

WHO ASSIST: identify exact version/language, electronic commercial administration,
class-specific scoring, follow-up pathway and report reproduction. Use the
[official resources](https://www.who.int/teams/mental-health-and-substance-use/alcohol-drugs-and-addictive-behaviours/assist)
and [WHO permissions process](https://www.who.int/about/policies/publishing/copyright).
TAPS and AUDIT already provide separate substance/alcohol options; do not treat
permission for one as permission for another.
