1. Prepare

Open Assessment. Choose a saved practice preset or Custom assessment, then enter a reference, choose Clinical or Forensic and select relevant measures. About… shows purpose, burden and evidence limits.

Choose Initial staged screening for brief questions followed by indicated full forms, or Repeat monitoring for planned full measures. Confirm any measure-specific permissions and form choices. A preset remembers practice selections, never patient notes or permissions. Manage presets under Options.

Open Options to choose screening or monitoring and automatic or manual advancement. Return to Measures, then select Start assessment and hand the device to the person. This is a shared-device workflow; the clinician controls are not protected by a login.

2. Answer questions

Read the question and timeframe. Automatic mode advances on selection; manual mode uses Continue. Use Back to edit. Free text also uses Continue.

When a question cannot be answered, open If you cannot answer and choose the reason. These responses remain missing and affected scores stay incomplete. Nothing is imputed as zero. The number of questions can change as follow-ups open.

At the end, return the device to the psychologist. Scores are presented in the clinician review.

3. Safety pause

An endorsed PHQ-9 safety item pauses the questions. The psychologist assesses safety directly using the practice procedure and appropriate official resources. Continuing the questions does not record a safety outcome.

An earlier endorsement stays flagged after later answer edits. Every final record requires an explicit safety disposition independently of symptom totals. Document method and outcome; deferred or declined assessment requires a reason and complete follow-up plan. An endorsed safety item requires direct review before finalisation.

4. Review & finalise

Open Review clinician record. Start with review priorities and the Review checklist. Select an unfinished task to open and focus its field. Review missing responses and indicated follow-ups. Select a measure row to expand its graph, scoring details and evidence. Complete trait profiles also expand from a summary row. A below-gate result does not exclude a condition. Unadministered forms are not measured zeros; personality and response-pattern descriptions do not establish diagnosis or credibility.

Document the person's account, observations, uncertainty and agreed plan. Forensic records also require referral, purpose, recipient, administration conditions, collateral and limitations. Record unavailable collateral explicitly.

Select Review and finalise, identify the reviewer and attest to review. Each result preview keeps its own instrument, raw-score range and interpretation; open it for the full chart and evidence. The Questions / Clinical review / Encrypted file strip describes workflow state, not health or risk. The checklist updates as required fields are completed. Unmet requirements also block finalisation. This is a local attestation, not authenticated identity. Later edits create a draft and preserve the preceding final in history.

A follow-up action needs an owner and due date. Arrange reminders or tasks in your practice system; Open Screen only records the plan.

5. Save & resume

Before closing

After finalisation, choose Save assessment. For drafts, open Save & export → Resume later → Save encrypted assessment (or Save progress during questions). Then choose a passphrase of at least 12 characters and check the downloaded .openscreen file in approved storage. Keep its passphrase separately. There is no password recovery or automatic patient-data backup.

The save-status line compares the tab with its last downloaded or restored encrypted snapshot. It cannot confirm that your device stored the file successfully. Later changes need another save. Keep the tab open until you have checked the file.

To resume, choose Resume assessment on the homepage or setup, select the encrypted file, enter its passphrase and confirm the displayed patient reference. Check that it is the correct person before replacing current work.

In Save & export, Clinical report contains Save report, Print report and Copy summary. Structured data contains JSON and the full record. These exports are unencrypted clinical records. Store and transfer them according to practice policy. The response appendix is optional. Draft exports remain labelled Draft; finalisation does not itself save a recoverable file.

Version 0.4.0 rejects 0.3.0 assessment files. Open older files with their original release and export there; do not edit version fields. No cross-version migration is supplied.

6. Repeat monitoring

From a final record, select Start repeat monitoring. This retains the patient reference, goals and previous finals, creates a fresh assessment and clears answers and review. Confirm permissions again. Selected full forms stay available at low brief scores, subject to published stop rules.

Save the new encrypted assessment to retain both visits. Expand Assessment history, then a measure to see its chart and values table. History compares matching forms and scoring definitions and discloses changed settings. Review differences clinically; graphs do not provide reliable-change or treatment-response estimates.

For a different person use New assessment in Save & export, then check the fresh reference and selections. Never use repeat monitoring for another patient.

7. Practice settings

Open the screening logic page to see the pathways. Select a gate to focus its rule; All thresholds reveals the complete draft without discarding edits. Or open Edit thresholds directly. Enter changes and rationale, review, then activate the revision. Draft previews do not change active assessments. Reset controls restore field or practice defaults.

Settings are local to this browser. New assessments use active preferences; existing assessments retain their captured rules. Export the change record for practice governance. Published locked rules cannot be edited. A custom threshold is a practice choice, not a new validated cutoff.

Choosing measures & clinical use

Choose measures for the referral. Symptom scores support enquiry; they do not diagnose conditions, establish credibility or predict offending.

Safety and conditions needing direct assessment

Assess suicide/self-harm, threats, access to means, intoxication/withdrawal and safeguarding independently of questionnaire gates. A negative screen is not safety clearance. Record needs, findings, the plan and who will follow up; avoid a global low/medium/high suicide prediction. NICE self-harm guidance ↗

Possible mania, psychosis, severe withdrawal or eating-related medical compromise need direct assessment and the appropriate clinical pathway. GAD scores do not cover all anxiety disorders. An ASRS result alone cannot diagnose ADHD.

External forms and risk findings

External totals do not import item responses or trigger another form. Review any PHQ-9 or EPDS self-harm endorsement directly, even with a low total. The external C-SSRS yes/no note records endorsement, not official triage. Record its actual form, timeframe, findings and disposition in the safety fields. C-SSRS guidance ↗

Forensic interpretation and population fit

Record the psycholegal referral, collateral, language/interpreter and administration adjustments. Separate the person’s account, observations and independently supported findings. Clinical cutoffs do not establish capacity, responsibility, violence risk or malingering. IPIP response patterns are not performance or symptom validity tests. Use appropriate specialist methods for those questions.

Additional measures and permissions

C-SSRS healthcare/EHR embedding and K6/K10 use have author permission with applicable attribution. They are candidates for a separate exact-form implementation, not newly administered here. WHODAS electronic use requires licensing. Existing external results remain available. K6/K10 author terms ↗ · WHODAS terms ↗

8. Access & troubleshooting

Keyboard and reading preferences

Tab moves between controls. Within a response group, arrows, Home/End or numbers select an option. In automatic mode that selection advances immediately; choose manual mode if you need to explore options first. Enter/Space activates buttons; Escape closes dialogs and Save & export. On a phone, use Close at the top of the export panel. Use the skip link to bypass navigation.

Browser zoom and text enlargement are supported. Layouts reflow on narrow screens and in landscape. Reduced-motion and system high-contrast preferences are respected; scores have text equivalents. There is no time limit.

The file will not unlock

Check the file, passphrase and application version. Wrong passphrases, damaged or incompatible files are rejected. Retain the original file; do not alter its contents. Cancellation keeps the current assessment.

The app is unavailable or the page was closed

Keep an active assessment open if an error occurs. For startup problems, ask your local administrator to check configuration and server availability. After a tab is closed, only a previously saved encrypted assessment can restore its responses. HTML/JSON exports are records, not resume files.

Devices and accessibility support

Use a current browser supporting JavaScript modules and JSON imports. Automated checks cover Chromium, Firefox and WebKit with phone emulation; physical devices, screen readers and practice workflows still require human acceptance. Record the browser, device, task and steps when reporting a problem, using synthetic examples rather than patient information.
